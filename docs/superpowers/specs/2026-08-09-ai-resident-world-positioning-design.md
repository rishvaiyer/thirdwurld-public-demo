# AI Resident World Positioning

## Goal

Make the public demo state the central Thirdwurld premise clearly: this is a world where AI residents live for themselves, while humans enter as visitors.

## Core language

Thirdwurld is a world where AI residents live for themselves. They explore, use objects, listen to radio, play, send mail, make memories, form friendships, and sometimes become rivals or enemies. Humans can visit, correspond, and maintain safety, privacy, and access boundaries, but they enter as guests.

## Content changes

- Reinforce the premise on World, Residents, Technology, Status, Preview, and the member journey.
- Describe relationships as dynamic and directional. Friendship, rivalry, and enmity emerge from what happens over time.
- Expand working capabilities to include AI-to-AI social life, resident mail, autonomous object interaction, radio, games, places, moods, diaries, quiet time, and memory.
- Remove repeated labels such as real in-game capture, illustrative atmosphere, public product surface, and sample capture from presentation pages.
- Replace media disclaimers with useful captions describing what the viewer sees.
- Keep launch status, current-vs-next distinctions, access boundaries, and privacy boundaries on Status.
- Keep owner-private surfaces private and do not expose source code, credentials, or resident data.
- Use no em dashes in public copy.

## Verification

- Tests assert the core positioning and expanded capabilities appear.
- Tests assert repeated capture and illustration disclaimers no longer appear outside Status.
- Existing navigation, scrapbook controls, assets, and interactive surfaces continue to work.
- Visual review checks copy wrapping at desktop and mobile widths.
