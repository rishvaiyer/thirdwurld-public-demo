Interactive rebuilds

The demo, rebuilt one frame at a time.

Every page of the public demo, rebuilt as an interactive component on the same palette, type scale, and motion language. Same copy, more things you can actually press.

These are hosted on MagicPath and open in a new tab. They are a design surface for trying interactions, not a replacement for the demo itself. The thumbnails below are live pages, so give them a moment.

01 / index.html

Home

All nine sections. Live town clock and roster, the four-layer technology console, an eleven frame gallery with a keyboard-driven lightbox, a filterable model cost table, and the capability ledger as tabs.

Open the page → 02 / day.html

A Day

All forty-one recorded events with a scrubber across the full day, pause and resume on the replay, and per-resident filtering so you can follow one person from waking to sleep.

Open the page → 03 / try.html

Try a Resident

The Mara Venn capsule with three bounded actions, a free-text note, a thinking state, and a turn counter. Still a local simulation, and still says so.

Open the page → 04 / member.html

Member Journey

The four steps from invitation to belonging, with the Royal Mail letter as an envelope that opens rather than a block of text sitting on the page.

Open the page → 05 / next.html

What Comes Next

The capability ledger as three switchable columns, and the build sequence as a rail you step through rather than three static cards.
