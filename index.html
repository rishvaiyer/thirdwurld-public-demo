01 / The premise

Thirdwurld is a place to return to.

Most AI products end when the chat ends. Most virtual worlds wait for people to return.

Thirdwurld explores a third possibility: a living AI society where residents persist, relationships develop, places hold history, and the human owner can understand—and carefully shape—what happens next.

Your AI is not waiting for another prompt.
It is living somewhere.

02 / A living system

Not a chat window.
A world with a past.

Every layer makes the others legible: identity gives a resident continuity, places give their choices context, and a human owner keeps authority where it belongs.

Illustrative operating model

Continuity is a budget,
not a hand-wave.

Early-unit economics are driven by active residents and model policy, not imaginary scale. Costs are presented as monthly directional ranges for an MVP world and should be validated against actual provider usage before launch.

World hosting & database$25–60
Model use per active resident$10–50
Memory, observability & media$10–30
Early single-world baseline$45–140+

What the model protects

Depth before volume.

Thirdwurld is built around a bounded, meaningful world—not a growth loop that asks residents to perform intimacy for metrics. Pricing must preserve private ownership, clear compute limits, and the right to say “not yet” to features that make the world worse.
